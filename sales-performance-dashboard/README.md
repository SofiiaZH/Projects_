# Sales Performance Dashboard

End-to-end sales performance analysis created in Google Sheets, covering revenue, profit, costs, products, geography, sales channels, shipping performance and sales trends.

The project transforms raw sales data into business metrics, analytical reports and an interactive dashboard to identify key performance drivers and support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Areas
- Revenue, profit and cost analysis
- Product category performance
- Country, region and subregion analysis
- Online vs offline sales channel analysis
- Shipping duration analysis
- Sales trends by year and month
- Profit analysis by day of the week
- ABC (Pareto) analysis
- KPI calculation
- Dashboard creation
- Data visualisation
- Business insights and recommendations

## Dashboard & Analysis
The project includes a dedicated business dashboard summarising key sales metrics and performance trends.

ABC analysis is used to classify product categories based on their contribution to overall business performance and identify the categories responsible for the majority of revenue and profit.

## Key Insights
- A small number of product categories generate a significant share of total revenue and profit
- Office Supplies, Cosmetics and Household are among the strongest-performing categories
- High revenue does not always translate into high profitability due to differences in cost structure
- Sales performance varies across countries, regions and sales channels
- Shipping duration and sales dynamics provide additional insight into operational performance
- Lower-performing categories present opportunities for cost and performance optimisation

## Tools
Google Sheets, Excel, Pivot Tables, Formulas, Data Analysis, Data Visualisation, Dashboarding, ABC Analysis
